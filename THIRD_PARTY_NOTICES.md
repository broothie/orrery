# Third-party data

## HYG Stellar Database

The generated `public/data/hyg-v4-mag7.bin` asset is derived from the HYG
Stellar Database v4.0 by David Nash. HYG combines data from Hipparcos, the Yale
Bright Star Catalog, and the Gliese Catalog of Nearby Stars.

Source: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database
